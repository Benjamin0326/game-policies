Tower Oops Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

1. App operator and privacy contact

Tower Oops (탑오정) is a game provided by Benjamin0326 Dev. For app support or privacy inquiries, including requests to access, correct, or delete personal information, contact benjamin0326.dev@gmail.com.

2. Game progress and on-device storage

The game stores progress, companion and equipment details, the player's display name, combat and play records, and settings on the device to enable gameplay, continuation, and saved preferences. The game does not provide accounts or sign-in, a developer-operated save server, or built-in cloud synchronization. It has no feature that sends this gameplay history to a developer-operated remote server.

The Android release candidate reviewed for this policy disables Android cloud backup of app data. Some Android devices may still copy app data during device-to-device migration. Clearing app storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app generally deletes data stored on that device. Copies transferred to another device must be managed separately on that device.

3. Optional rewarded ads and Google SDKs

The game includes an optional rewarded-ad feature and integrates the Google Mobile Ads Next-Gen SDK and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK. Whether ads are available depends on the app version and service readiness. In the first closed-testing Release candidate reviewed for this policy, app-initiated ad initialization and requests, and UMP consent update requests, are disabled, so ads are not displayed. Disabling ad requests does not establish that bundled SDKs perform no data processing.

Google's published information about the Mobile Ads Next-Gen SDK describes data that may be collected and shared with Google depending on SDK behavior and configuration, including:

  • IP addresses: for connectivity and potentially estimating approximate location
  • Device and app identifiers: Android advertising ID, app set ID, and other applicable identifiers
  • App and ad interactions: app launches, ad views, and user interactions
  • Diagnostics: app and SDK performance, startup time, and error-related information

Such information may be used to deliver ads, measure and analyze ad performance, prevent fraud, and protect security. Google states that data transmitted by the Mobile Ads Next-Gen SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. For further details, including Google's retention and deletion practices, see the Google Privacy Policy and Google ads SDK data disclosure. The disclosure describes Google's latest SDK and may not exactly reflect the installed version and configuration in this app.

Where applicable, privacy choices and consent for advertising will be presented according to the app version, user region, and legal requirements. You can reset or delete the Android advertising ID in device settings. Watching ads is not required for ordinary gameplay.

4. Support emails

If you voluntarily email support, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond and resolve the issue. Messages are retained as necessary to handle inquiries and deleted after that purpose has been fulfilled, unless a legal retention requirement applies, in which case records are kept for the legally required period and then deleted. Email service providers may retain information according to their own policies.

To request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of inquiry-related personal data, email the address above. We will assess and respond to requests as required by applicable law. Support email may be processed through the sender's email provider and Google's email services.

5. Intended audience and minors

Tower Oops is intended for players aged 13 and older. The game does not provide an in-app feature for entering a date of birth. In South Korea, processing the personal information of users under 14 may require consent from a legal representative and verification of that consent under applicable law. Before enabling advertising in future releases, applicable age- and region-specific privacy, consent, and protection requirements must be assessed and the necessary safeguards applied. Teen players and legal representatives may contact the email address above.

6. Security and external services

Game progress is generally processed on the device. When advertising and consent features are used, Google may process data as the provider of the relevant SDKs. Support requests are processed through email providers. This policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log and store visitor IP addresses for security. The website itself does not add analytics scripts or tracking cookies.

External service providers may process information using infrastructure located outside South Korea, subject to their own policies and applicable law. You can also read the GitHub Privacy Statement. If data processing purposes or practices change, this policy and the app's Google Play Data safety disclosures will be reviewed and updated.

7. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when app features, integrated SDKs, or legal obligations change. Updated terms and the effective date will be posted on this page.
