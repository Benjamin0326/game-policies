Game data and on-device storage

Tower Oops (탑오정) stores progress, companion information, the player display name, play records and settings on your device so you can resume play. The app has no account or sign-in feature, no developer-operated server and no built-in cloud sync.

Android backup and local data deletion

Clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app generally removes app data stored on that device. Depending on the app's backup configuration, device settings and Android version, the system may automatically back up eligible app data or transfer it between devices. If an earlier backup remains, data may be restored when you reinstall the app or set up a new device. Which game data is eligible for backup or transfer in this release candidate is still under review.

Google advertising and consent SDKs

The Android closed-test candidate reviewed for this draft includes Google Mobile Ads Next-Gen SDK (GMA) 1.6.0 and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK 4.0.0. This candidate disables app-initiated advertising SDK initialization, ad requests and UMP consent information update requests.

The app's Android manifest contains components that can be used for startup initialization and declares permissions related to advertising identifiers. Whether the SDKs initialize automatically, access identifiers or transmit data over the network in this candidate is still under review.

Google's disclosure for the latest GMA Next-Gen SDK describes IP addresses (which may be used to estimate approximate location), device and account-related identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostics as data used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. For the SDK versions and configuration included in Tower Oops, the data actually collected and shared, purposes, recipients, user choices, safeguards, retention and deletion are still under review.

Support and privacy requests

If you email support, your email address and the contents of your message may be used to respond. You can also send privacy questions and requests to the address above. Safeguards, retention and deletion periods and methods, and procedures for exercising privacy rights are still under review.
