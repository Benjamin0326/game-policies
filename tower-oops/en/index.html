Game data and on-device storage

Tower Oops (탑오정) stores progress, companion information, the player display name, play records and settings on your device so you can resume play. The game does not offer accounts, sign-in, developer-hosted saves or cloud sync. Clearing the app's data in Android settings or uninstalling the app deletes its game data and settings on that device.

Google advertising and consent SDKs

The Android app includes the Google Mobile Ads Next-Gen SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). The closed-test candidate reviewed for this draft disables ad requests and UMP consent requests initiated by the app. Automatic SDK initialization, identifier access and actual network transmission are pending confirmation.

Google's guidance for its latest advertising SDK describes how IP addresses (which may be used to estimate approximate location), device and account-related identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostics may be used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. For the SDK version and release configuration used by this app, the actual data types, purposes, recipients, user choices, security, retention and deletion are pending confirmation.
